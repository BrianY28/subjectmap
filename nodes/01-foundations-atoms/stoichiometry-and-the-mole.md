# Stoichiometry and the mole

Stoichiometry uses balanced chemical equations to relate amounts consumed and produced. The mole connects a count of specified entities to laboratory-scale quantities.

## Concepts

- **Amount of substance**: One mole contains exactly 6.02214076 × 10²³ specified entities, such as atoms, molecules or ions.
- **Balanced equations**: Stoichiometric coefficients conserve each chemical element and electric charge in a reaction equation.
- **Limiting reactant**: The reactant exhausted first fixes the maximum product amount for the assumed reaction and complete conversion.
- **Yield**: Actual isolated product can fall below the theoretical amount because of incomplete conversion, competing reactions and losses.
