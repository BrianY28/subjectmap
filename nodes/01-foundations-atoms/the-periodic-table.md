# The periodic table

Arranging elements by atomic number reveals recurring patterns related to valence-electron structure. Trends guide predictions but do not replace evidence for a particular substance.

## Concepts

- **Periods and groups**: Rows track successive shells and columns collect elements with recurring valence patterns.
- **Atomic size**: Effective nuclear attraction and shielding help explain broad size trends across and down the table.
- **Ionization energy**: Removing an electron requires energy that depends on its binding and the electronic structure of the resulting ion.
- **Chemical context**: Oxidation states and reactivity depend on bonding partners and conditions, so periodic trends have meaningful exceptions.
