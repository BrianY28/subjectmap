# Acids and bases

Acid-base models describe proton transfer or electron-pair donation. Their predictions depend on the solvent, chemical species and equilibrium conditions.

## Concepts

- **Brønsted-Lowry model**: An acid donates a proton and a base accepts one, creating conjugate acid-base pairs.
- **Lewis model**: A Lewis acid accepts an electron pair and a Lewis base donates one to form an interaction or bond.
- **Acidity and pH**: pH is defined using hydrogen-ion activity; replacing activity with concentration is an approximation for sufficiently dilute solutions.
- **Buffers**: A weak acid and its conjugate base can resist limited additions of acid or base, with effectiveness constrained by composition and capacity.
