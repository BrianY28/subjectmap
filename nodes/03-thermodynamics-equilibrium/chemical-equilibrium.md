# Chemical equilibrium

At equilibrium, opposing reaction rates balance and macroscopic composition remains constant under fixed conditions. Equilibrium composition need not contain equal amounts of reactants and products.

## Concepts

- **Dynamic balance**: Microscopic forward and reverse reactions continue even when there is no net macroscopic reaction.
- **Mass action**: For a specified reaction and standard states, the dimensionless activity quotient Q equals K at equilibrium. [Explainer](../../explainers/equilibrium/)
- **Reaction direction**: At a given temperature, Q below K favors the forward direction and Q above K favors the reverse direction under the usual closed-system constraints.
- **Changing conditions**: Temperature changes K, while adding material at fixed temperature changes Q and can shift the equilibrium composition.
