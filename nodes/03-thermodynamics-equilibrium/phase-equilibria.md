# Phase equilibria

Different phases coexist when their components have matching chemical potentials under the imposed conditions. Phase diagrams organize the stable states and boundaries of a system.

## Concepts

- **Coexistence curves**: Lines in a one-component pressure-temperature diagram mark conditions where two phases coexist.
- **Triple and critical points**: A triple point permits three-phase coexistence, while a liquid-gas critical point ends their coexistence boundary.
- **Vapor-liquid balance**: At equilibrium, evaporation and condensation balance; vapor pressure depends on temperature and composition.
- **Mixture effects**: Mixing changes chemical potentials, producing effects such as freezing-point depression under the applicable dilute-solution assumptions.
