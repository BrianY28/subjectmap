# Catalysis

A catalyst participates in a reaction pathway and is regenerated in its overall cycle. It can change reaction speed and selectivity without changing the equilibrium constant for the same overall reaction.

## Concepts

- **Alternative pathway**: Catalytic steps offer a different route with favorable kinetic barriers compared with an uncatalyzed pathway.
- **Equilibrium unchanged**: Under fixed conditions a catalyst accelerates approach to equilibrium but does not alter the reaction’s thermodynamic equilibrium constant.
- **Homogeneous and heterogeneous**: A catalyst may share the reactants’ phase or act at an interface between phases.
- **Turnover and selectivity**: Useful catalytic performance depends on rate, preferred products and lifetime, including deactivation and competing pathways.
