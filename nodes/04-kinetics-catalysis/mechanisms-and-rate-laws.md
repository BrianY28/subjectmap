# Mechanisms and rate laws

A mechanism proposes elementary steps whose sum gives the observed reaction. Agreement with one rate law supports a mechanism but rarely identifies it uniquely.

## Concepts

- **Elementary steps**: Molecularity counts reacting entities in a proposed elementary event rather than in the overall balanced equation.
- **Intermediates**: An intermediate is formed and consumed along the pathway and therefore cancels from the overall reaction equation.
- **Kinetic approximations**: Pre-equilibrium or steady-state approximations simplify rate equations only when their timescale assumptions hold.
- **Testing a mechanism**: Rate measurements, isotope effects, spectroscopy and product distributions provide complementary constraints on proposed steps.
