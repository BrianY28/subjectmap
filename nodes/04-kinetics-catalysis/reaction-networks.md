# Reaction networks

Linked reactions couple species through shared intermediates and conservation laws. Nonlinear feedback and external driving can produce behavior absent from a single isolated reaction.

## Concepts

- **Stoichiometric network**: A stoichiometric matrix records how each reaction changes each species and identifies conserved combinations.
- **Coupled kinetics**: Rate equations combine individual reaction rates to determine the time evolution of all species together.
- **Driven states**: Open systems supplied with material or energy can sustain fluxes, multiple steady states or oscillations far from equilibrium.
- **Molecular fluctuations**: For well-mixed systems with small populations, stochastic reaction models can capture fluctuations missed by deterministic concentration equations.
