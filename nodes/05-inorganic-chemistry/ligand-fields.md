# Ligand fields

Ligands alter the energies and mixing of metal orbitals. Ligand-field models connect electronic structure to spectra, spin states and reactivity.

## Concepts

- **Orbital splitting**: Different coordination geometries produce different energy patterns among the metal d orbitals.
- **Spin states**: The balance between orbital splitting and electron-pairing costs helps determine high-spin or low-spin arrangements where both are possible.
- **Spectroscopic transitions**: Electronic transitions can produce absorption bands, although charge-transfer processes also contribute strongly in many complexes.
- **Beyond crystal fields**: Ligand-field molecular-orbital descriptions include covalent interactions that a purely electrostatic crystal-field model omits.
