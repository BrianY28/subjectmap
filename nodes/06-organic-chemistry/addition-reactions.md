# Addition reactions

Addition transforms a multiple bond by attaching groups across it. Mechanism determines which positions and faces react.

## Concepts

- **Electrophilic addition**: An electron-rich π bond can react with an electrophile, followed by additional bonding steps.
- **Regioselectivity**: Unsymmetrical substrates can give different constitutional products, with preference determined by the particular pathway.
- **Stereoselectivity**: Syn and anti addition describe whether new groups are delivered to the same or opposite faces of a reacting π system.
- **Catalytic hydrogenation**: A catalyst can mediate net hydrogen addition across an unsaturated bond, with selectivity influenced by catalyst and substrate.
