# Aromaticity and substitution

Aromaticity describes characteristic stabilization and electronic behavior in certain cyclic conjugated systems. Their reactions often preserve or restore the conjugated ring.

## Concepts

- **Hückel pattern**: For simple planar cyclic conjugated systems, 4n + 2 π electrons is a useful aromaticity criterion within the model’s limits.
- **Delocalization**: Aromatic bonding is distributed around the ring rather than alternating between localized drawings over time.
- **Electrophilic substitution**: Many aromatic rings react by temporary loss of aromaticity followed by elimination that restores it.
- **Substituent effects**: Attached groups change reaction rates and positional preferences through electronic and steric effects.
