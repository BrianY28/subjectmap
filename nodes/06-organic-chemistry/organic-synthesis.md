# Organic synthesis

Synthesis plans a sequence of transformations that builds a target molecule with the required structure and purity. Successful routes balance selectivity with practical constraints.

## Concepts

- **Retrosynthesis**: Working backward from a target identifies simpler precursors and candidate bond-forming transformations.
- **Chemoselectivity**: A reaction must distinguish the intended functional group from other reactive groups in a molecule.
- **Stereocontrol**: A route must account for the creation, preservation or separation of the required stereoisomers.
- **Route evaluation**: Yield, step count, separations, waste and reproducibility all affect whether a proposed sequence is useful.
