# Substitution and elimination

Substitution replaces a group, while elimination creates a multiple bond by removing groups. Competing mechanisms depend on substrate, reagent and reaction environment.

## Concepts

- **SN2 substitution**: A concerted displacement involves nucleophilic attack and departure of the leaving group, with inversion at the attacked tetrahedral carbon.
- **SN1 substitution**: Ionization can form a carbocation intermediate whose stability and subsequent reactions influence rate and products.
- **E2 elimination**: A concerted elimination couples proton removal to leaving-group departure and has important geometric requirements.
- **Competition**: Basicity, nucleophilicity, solvent and steric effects influence competition among substitution and elimination pathways.
