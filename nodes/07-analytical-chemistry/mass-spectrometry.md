# Mass spectrometry

Mass spectrometry measures ions by their mass-to-charge ratio, m/z. Ionization, isotope patterns and fragmentation provide evidence about composition and structure rather than an automatic unique identification.

## Concepts

- **Ionization**: Methods differ in the ions and fragments they produce, and a neutral molecule is not detected directly.
- **Mass-to-charge ratio**: An observed peak depends on both ion mass and charge, including adduct formation and multiple charging.
- **Isotopes and accurate mass**: Resolved masses and isotope patterns constrain elemental composition, but different structures can share the same formula.
- **Tandem mass spectrometry**: Selecting and fragmenting an ion reveals additional structural evidence that can be compared with standards or spectral libraries.
