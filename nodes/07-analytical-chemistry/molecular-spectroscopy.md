# Molecular spectroscopy

Molecules absorb, emit or scatter radiation through transitions between energy levels. Spectral position and intensity connect molecular structure and environment to measurable signals.

## Concepts

- **Vibrational spectra**: Infrared absorption and Raman scattering probe vibrations through different selection rules and can provide complementary structural evidence.
- **Electronic transitions**: UV-visible spectra probe electronic excitations whose energies depend on bonding, conjugation and environment.
- **Beer-Lambert relation**: A = εlc relates absorbance to concentration for suitable dilute, homogeneous samples at a specified wavelength, with deviations outside its assumptions.
- **Spectral interpretation**: Bands can overlap and shift with conditions, so assigning a structure usually requires multiple observations.
