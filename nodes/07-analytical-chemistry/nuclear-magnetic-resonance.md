# Nuclear magnetic resonance

NMR observes transitions of spin-active nuclei in a magnetic field. Chemical shifts, couplings and correlations help determine molecular environments, connectivity and dynamics.

## Concepts

- **Chemical shift**: Electronic shielding changes a nucleus's resonance position relative to a reference, conventionally reported in parts per million.
- **Spin-spin coupling**: Interactions between nuclear spins can split signals and reveal connectivity, although simple splitting rules have limited domains.
- **Signal integration**: Under suitable acquisition and relaxation conditions, integrated proton signals estimate relative numbers of contributing nuclei.
- **Multidimensional NMR**: Correlations between nuclei help disentangle overlapping signals and establish structural relationships.
