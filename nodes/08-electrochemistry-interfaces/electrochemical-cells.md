# Electrochemical cells

A cell separates coupled electrode reactions and links ionic transport through an electrolyte with electron flow through an external circuit. Chemical free energy can drive current, or electrical work can drive a reaction.

## Concepts

- **Anode and cathode**: Oxidation occurs at the anode and reduction at the cathode in both galvanic and electrolytic operation.
- **Charge balance**: Electrolyte ion movement prevents sustained charge buildup that would stop cell operation.
- **Cell potential**: With both half-cell values written as reduction potentials, E_cell = E_cathode − E_anode.
- **Reversible work**: For a reversible cell reaction at fixed temperature and pressure, ΔᵣG = −nFE, with n electrons transferred per reaction as written.
