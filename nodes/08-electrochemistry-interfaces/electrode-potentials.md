# Electrode potentials

Electrode potentials are measured relative to a reference electrode and depend on chemical conditions. Equilibrium potentials describe thermodynamic driving forces, while operating electrodes also show kinetic and transport losses.

## Concepts

- **Reference scales**: A reported electrode potential needs its reference electrode and conditions to be interpretable.
- **Nernst equation**: The reversible cell potential obeys E = E° − (RT/nF) ln Q with dimensionless activities; at overall reaction equilibrium, E = 0.
- **Standard conditions**: Standard potentials refer to standard-state activities at a stated temperature, not every solution with a nominal concentration of one molar.
- **Overpotential**: Driving a finite electrode reaction rate generally requires a potential displaced from its equilibrium value.
