# Surfaces and colloids

Interfaces have structures and energies that differ from the bulk. Small particles and droplets expose large interfacial areas, making adsorption and intermolecular forces central to colloidal behavior.

## Concepts

- **Surface free energy**: Creating an interface generally costs free energy, which helps explain droplet shapes and aggregation tendencies.
- **Adsorption**: Accumulation of species at an interface can alter wetting, reaction rates and effective surface charge.
- **Colloidal stability**: Electrostatic repulsion, attractive forces and steric barriers can oppose or promote aggregation.
- **Surfactant assembly**: Amphiphilic molecules can form micelles or other assemblies when their molecular interactions and solution conditions favor them.
