# Chemical probes

Chemical probes perturb or label biological systems to test molecular hypotheses. A useful probe requires evidence of target engagement and selectivity in the context where it is used.

## Concepts

- **Target engagement**: Binding or reaction with the intended target must be established rather than inferred solely from a cellular response.
- **Selectivity**: Activity against unintended targets can confound interpretation, especially as concentration or exposure changes.
- **Controls**: Inactive analogues, independent probes and complementary biological approaches help distinguish target effects from artifacts.
- **Bioorthogonal labeling**: Selected reactions can tag biomolecules in complex biological settings while minimizing reaction with native functional groups.
