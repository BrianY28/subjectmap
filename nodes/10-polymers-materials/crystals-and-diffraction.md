# Crystals and diffraction

Diffraction records the interference of waves scattered by an ordered material. Structural models connect diffraction patterns to atomic arrangements while accounting for symmetry, disorder and experimental uncertainty.

## Concepts

- **Unit cells and symmetry**: A periodic crystal can be described by a repeating unit cell and the symmetry relating equivalent positions.
- **Bragg relation**: Constructive reflection from lattice planes satisfies nλ = 2d sin θ for the stated diffraction geometry.
- **Structure refinement**: Measured intensities constrain a structural model rather than providing a direct photograph of atoms.
- **Defects and disorder**: Vacancies, substitutions and imperfect ordering affect both diffraction signals and material properties.
