# Nanochemistry

At nanometre scales, large surface fractions and confinement can change a material's properties. Size, shape and surface chemistry are therefore part of the chemical identity of a nanoparticle sample.

## Concepts

- **Surface-to-volume ratio**: A larger fraction of atoms lies near an interface as particle size decreases, amplifying surface effects.
- **Quantum confinement**: In sufficiently small semiconductor particles, confinement changes electronic energy levels and optical response.
- **Surface ligands**: Bound molecules can stabilize particles, influence growth and change interactions with their surroundings.
- **Size distributions**: Measured properties reflect an ensemble of sizes and shapes, so a nominal mean size is not a complete description.
