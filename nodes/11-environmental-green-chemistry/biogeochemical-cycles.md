# Biogeochemical cycles

Elements move among geological, atmospheric and biological reservoirs through linked physical and chemical processes. Human activity can change both the sizes of reservoirs and the rates connecting them.

## Concepts

- **Reservoirs and fluxes**: A large stored quantity and a large transfer rate describe different aspects of an elemental cycle.
- **Carbon transformations**: Photosynthesis, respiration, dissolution and mineral formation redistribute carbon among compounds and reservoirs.
- **Nitrogen transformations**: Fixation, nitrification and denitrification change nitrogen's chemical form and biological availability.
- **Nutrient imbalance**: Added nitrogen or phosphorus can change ecosystem growth and oxygen demand, connecting chemical inputs to ecological effects.
