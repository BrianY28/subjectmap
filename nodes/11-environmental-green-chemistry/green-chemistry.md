# Green chemistry

Green chemistry designs products and processes to reduce hazardous substances and waste at their source. A greener choice needs evidence across the process rather than a single favorable label.

## Concepts

- **Waste prevention**: Avoiding unwanted material is preferable to relying solely on treatment after it forms.
- **Atom economy**: The stoichiometric mass fraction of reactants incorporated into the desired product complements yield but does not capture solvents, energy or toxicity.
- **Safer design**: Choosing less hazardous reagents, solvents and products can reduce risks throughout their use and disposal.
- **Whole-process assessment**: Energy demand, feedstock production, separation, reuse and end-of-life outcomes can change the comparison between routes.
