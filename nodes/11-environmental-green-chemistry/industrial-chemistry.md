# Industrial chemistry

Industrial chemistry turns reactions into dependable manufacturing processes by integrating catalysis, separation, energy and material flows. A successful laboratory reaction may need substantial redesign for reliable production.

## Concepts

- **Reaction and separation**: Unreacted feed and by-products must be separated, recovered or treated as part of the complete process.
- **Haber-Bosch synthesis**: Catalytic ammonia production combines nitrogen and hydrogen, balancing equilibrium, reaction rate and process integration.
- **Process intensification**: Changing equipment or integrating operations can improve heat transfer, mass transfer and resource efficiency.
- **Feedstock and energy choices**: The environmental footprint depends on how raw materials and energy are supplied, not only on the final reaction vessel.
