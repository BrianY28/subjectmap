# Water chemistry

Water chemistry depends on dissolved gases, ions, organic matter and interactions with solids. Chemical speciation often matters as much as the total amount of an element.

## Concepts

- **pH and speciation**: Acid-base equilibria control the distribution of protonated forms and can change solubility and reactivity.
- **Alkalinity**: Acid-neutralizing capacity is distinct from pH and commonly reflects bicarbonate and carbonate in natural waters.
- **Dissolution and precipitation**: Mineral equilibria, complex formation and kinetics determine whether elements remain dissolved or enter solids.
- **Redox conditions**: Oxygen availability and other electron acceptors influence microbial pathways and the chemical forms of dissolved elements.
