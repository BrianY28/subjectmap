# Predictive synthesis

Computer-assisted synthesis now predicts reactions and proposes routes from large chemical datasets. Reliable planning still requires testing unfamiliar chemistry, practical conditions and whether an entire proposed route can be executed.

## Concepts

- **Retrosynthetic search**: Work backward from a target to candidate precursors, then assess the feasibility of each proposed transformation.
- **Recent demonstration**: RetroChimera (2026) reports improved predictions under tested distribution shifts and chemist assessments, without establishing universal chemical reliability.
- **Data and evaluation**: Published reactions omit many failures, and benchmark overlap or narrow coverage can make performance appear more general than it is.
- **Open question**: Can a system predict conditions, selectivity and connected multistep routes with calibrated uncertainty and prospective experimental success?
