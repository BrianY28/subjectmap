# Sustainable chemical energy

Electrochemical and photochemical routes can convert energy into chemical fuels and feedstocks. The open challenge is to combine selective chemistry with durable operation and a favorable complete energy and material balance.

## Concepts

- **Selectivity and efficiency**: High Faradaic efficiency identifies where charge goes, but a process can still consume excessive energy at its operating voltage.
- **Device-level demonstrations**: CO₂ electrolyzers can achieve useful product formation under specific conditions, while catalyst and reactor design jointly determine performance.
- **Durability and resources**: Long operation, impurity tolerance and accessible catalyst materials matter alongside a short high-performance measurement.
- **Open question**: Can a complete process, including feed preparation and product separation, provide a scalable low-emission alternative under realistic electricity supplies?
