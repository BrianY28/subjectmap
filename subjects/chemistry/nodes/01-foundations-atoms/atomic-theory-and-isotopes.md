# Atomic theory and isotopes

Atoms are identified by their proton number; isotopes of an element differ in neutron number. Atomic theory links composition measurements to microscopic structure.

## Concepts

- **Atomic number**: The number of protons identifies an element; changing electron number changes its ionic charge.
- **Isotopes**: Isotopes share atomic number but differ in mass number because they contain different numbers of neutrons.
- **Atomic weights**: An element’s atomic weight reflects isotopic masses and abundances, which can vary among natural samples.
- **Chemical conservation**: Ordinary chemical reactions rearrange nuclei and electrons without changing the identities of the nuclei.
