# Chemical measurement

Chemical claims become testable when quantities, units and uncertainty are reported together. Measurements connect macroscopic observations to models of matter.

## Concepts

- **Units and dimensions**: A numerical value needs a unit, and dimensional analysis checks whether an equation compares compatible quantities.
- **Accuracy and precision**: Agreement with a reference and agreement among repeated measurements describe different aspects of measurement quality.
- **Uncertainty**: An uncertainty estimate describes a range of plausible values under stated assumptions, rather than a guarantee of exactness.
- **Calibration**: Reference materials and calibration measurements connect instrument response to the quantity being measured.
