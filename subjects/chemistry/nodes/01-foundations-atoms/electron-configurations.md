# Electron configurations

Quantum mechanics describes electrons using states rather than fixed planetary orbits. Electron configurations summarize orbital occupancies and help explain chemical trends.

## Concepts

- **Orbitals**: An orbital is a one-electron wavefunction used in an approximate description of a many-electron atom.
- **Pauli exclusion**: At most two electrons occupy one spatial orbital, with opposite spin projections.
- **Hund’s rule**: For degenerate orbitals in the usual ground-state filling picture, parallel singly occupied states are favored before pairing.
- **Configuration limits**: Simple filling orders are useful patterns with exceptions, and ionization can change the ordering and occupancy of orbitals.
