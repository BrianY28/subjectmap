# States and mixtures

Matter can be classified by physical state and composition. Pure substances, solutions and heterogeneous mixtures require different descriptions and separation methods.

## Concepts

- **Phases**: A phase is a region with uniform physical and chemical properties, separated from other phases by an interface.
- **Pure substances**: Elements and compounds have defined chemical identities, unlike mixtures whose component proportions can vary.
- **Solutions**: A solution is a homogeneous mixture; specifying concentration requires stating the amount and reference basis.
- **Physical separation**: Differences in volatility, solubility or particle size can separate mixture components without changing their chemical identities.
