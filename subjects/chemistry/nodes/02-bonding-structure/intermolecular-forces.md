# Intermolecular forces

Interactions between molecules help determine boiling, solubility and organization. Their combined effects depend on molecular shape, polarizability and the surrounding medium.

## Concepts

- **Dispersion**: Correlated fluctuations in electron distributions produce attractive dispersion interactions in all atoms and molecules.
- **Dipolar interactions**: Permanent dipoles interact in ways that depend on orientation and separation.
- **Hydrogen bonding**: A hydrogen attached to a suitable donor can interact directionally with an acceptor, influencing structure and association.
- **Collective properties**: Many competing interactions and entropy determine bulk behavior, so one bond type alone rarely predicts solubility.
