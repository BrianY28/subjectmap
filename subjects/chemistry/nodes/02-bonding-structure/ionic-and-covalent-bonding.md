# Ionic and covalent bonding

Chemical bonding arises when interactions between nuclei and electrons stabilize an arrangement. Ionic and covalent descriptions are useful limiting models of a wider range of electron distributions.

## Concepts

- **Covalent bonding**: Shared electron density can stabilize a pair of nuclei at a finite equilibrium separation.
- **Ionic solids**: Electrostatic interactions extend throughout an ionic lattice, so its stability is not determined by one isolated ion pair.
- **Bond polarity**: Unequal electron sharing creates partial charges; these need not equal formal charges or oxidation states.
- **Bond energy**: Breaking a bond requires energy, while forming the corresponding bond releases energy under the same reference conditions.
