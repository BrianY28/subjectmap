# Lewis structures and resonance

Lewis structures track valence electrons and provide a first model of bonding. Resonance describes a single electronic structure using multiple contributing drawings.

## Concepts

- **Electron accounting**: Valence electrons are assigned to bonds and lone pairs while preserving the species’ total electron count.
- **Formal charge**: Formal charge assigns bonding electrons equally and is a bookkeeping device rather than a direct measurement of charge density.
- **Resonance contributors**: Different electron-placement drawings with the same nuclear connectivity describe delocalization, not molecules rapidly switching structures.
- **Octet limits**: Electron-deficient species, radicals and some heavier-element compounds require descriptions beyond a simple octet rule.
