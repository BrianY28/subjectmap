# Molecular geometry

The spatial arrangement of nuclei influences polarity and reactivity. VSEPR gives a useful qualitative starting point for many main-group molecules.

## Concepts

- **Electron domains**: VSEPR counts bonding regions and lone pairs around a central atom to estimate an electron-domain arrangement.
- **Molecular shape**: Names such as bent or trigonal pyramidal describe nuclear positions rather than lone-pair positions.
- **Molecular dipole**: Bond-dipole vectors may reinforce or cancel according to the whole molecule’s geometry.
- **Model boundaries**: Transition-metal compounds and strongly delocalized systems often need more detailed electronic-structure models.
