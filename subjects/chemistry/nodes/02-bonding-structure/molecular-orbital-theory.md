# Molecular orbital theory

Molecular orbitals extend across a molecule and are filled with electrons subject to quantum rules. This model explains features such as delocalization and the magnetism of oxygen.

## Concepts

- **Orbital combinations**: Atomic-orbital combinations can form bonding, antibonding and nonbonding molecular orbitals.
- **Bond order**: In a simple diatomic model, half the difference between bonding and antibonding occupancies estimates bond order.
- **Magnetism**: Unpaired electrons in molecular orbitals give a paramagnetic response, as in ground-state O₂.
- **Approximation**: Orbital pictures are model-dependent representations; quantitative predictions also require electron correlation and appropriate computational methods.
