# Stereochemistry

Molecules with identical connectivity can differ in their spatial arrangements. Such differences matter when molecules encounter chiral biological or chemical environments.

## Concepts

- **Chirality**: A chiral object is not superimposable on its mirror image; chirality need not arise from a single tetrahedral stereocenter.
- **Enantiomers and diastereomers**: Enantiomers are non-superimposable mirror images, while diastereomers are stereoisomers that are not mirror images.
- **Configuration and conformation**: Conformers interconvert through motions such as single-bond rotation, while configuration distinguishes stereoisomers beyond those motions.
- **Stereochemical labels**: R/S and E/Z encode structural relationships and do not by themselves specify the sign of optical rotation.
