# Energy and enthalpy

Energy conservation connects chemical changes to heat and work. Enthalpy is useful for comparing processes carried out at constant pressure.

## Concepts

- **First law**: With heat entering the system and work done on it defined positive, the internal-energy change is ΔU = q + w.
- **State functions**: Internal energy and enthalpy depend on state, whereas heat and work depend on the process path.
- **Enthalpy**: H = U + pV, and at constant pressure with only pressure-volume work, the heat transferred equals ΔH.
- **Hess’s law**: Reaction enthalpies can be added along a hypothetical sequence because the total enthalpy change is path-independent.
