# Entropy and free energy

Entropy accounts for the distribution of energy and accessible microscopic states. Gibbs energy combines energetic and entropic contributions under constant-temperature, constant-pressure conditions.

## Concepts

- **Entropy**: For an isolated system, spontaneous change increases total entropy; the entropy of one subsystem can decrease.
- **Gibbs energy**: G = H − TS supplies the equilibrium criterion for a closed system at fixed temperature and pressure with no imposed non-expansion work.
- **Reaction Gibbs energy**: ΔᵣG = ΔᵣG° + RT ln Q relates the local driving force to the dimensionless activity quotient.
- **Thermodynamics versus kinetics**: A negative reaction Gibbs energy favors a direction of change but does not guarantee an observable rate.
