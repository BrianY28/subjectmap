# Solubility and complex equilibria

Dissolution, precipitation and complex formation are coupled equilibria. Predicting dissolved amounts requires accounting for all important species and conservation constraints.

## Concepts

- **Solubility product**: Ksp relates ion activities at equilibrium with a specified solid phase, whose activity is normally taken as one when pure.
- **Common-ion effect**: Adding a shared ion often suppresses dissolution, but competing complexation or other equilibria can change the outcome.
- **Complex formation**: Ligands can reduce a free metal-ion activity by forming dissolved complexes, altering solubility and speciation.
- **Mass and charge balance**: Equilibrium relations must be solved consistently with component balances and electroneutrality.
