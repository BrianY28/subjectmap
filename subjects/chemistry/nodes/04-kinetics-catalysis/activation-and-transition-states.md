# Activation and transition states

Reaction rates depend on access to high-energy configurations along a pathway. Barrier models explain why a favorable overall reaction can remain slow.

## Concepts

- **Arrhenius behavior**: The approximate relation k = A exp(−Ea/RT) connects a rate constant to temperature over a suitable range.
- **Transition state**: A transition state lies near a dividing region between reactants and products and is not an isolable intermediate.
- **Activation free energy**: Transition-state theory relates rate to a free-energy barrier, including entropic as well as energetic contributions.
- **Model limits**: Tunneling, recrossing, diffusion and changes of mechanism can cause departures from simple barrier-based rate formulas.
