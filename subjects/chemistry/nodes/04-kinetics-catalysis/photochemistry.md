# Photochemistry

Absorbed light prepares electronically excited states that can react differently from ground-state molecules. Chemical outcomes compete with relaxation and emission.

## Concepts

- **Photoexcitation**: A photon can raise a molecule into an accessible excited state when its energy and transition properties permit absorption.
- **Competing pathways**: Fluorescence, nonradiative relaxation, intersystem crossing and chemical reaction compete after excitation.
- **Quantum yield**: The quantum yield counts specified events per absorbed photon; it must identify the event and illumination conditions.
- **Light-driven chemistry**: Photochemical reactions exchange energy with a light field, so their operating states need not equal dark thermal equilibrium.
