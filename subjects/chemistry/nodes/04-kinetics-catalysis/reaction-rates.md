# Reaction rates

Kinetics describes how rapidly composition changes. A rate law summarizes observed dependence on concentrations and conditions within its range of validity.

## Concepts

- **Rate definition**: Changes in species concentration are divided by stoichiometric coefficients to define a consistent reaction rate at fixed volume.
- **Reaction order**: Concentration exponents in an empirical rate law are measured and need not equal coefficients in the overall equation.
- **Integrated laws**: A kinetic model can be integrated to predict concentration against time, with different forms for different reaction orders.
- **Experimental control**: Temperature, mixing, transport and measurement response must be considered when interpreting observed rates.
