# Bioinorganic chemistry

Biological systems use metal ions for structural, catalytic and electron-transfer functions. The surrounding ligands and protein environment tune a metal site’s behavior.

## Concepts

- **Metalloproteins**: A protein can position a metal ion and ligands to support a specific chemical function.
- **Small-molecule binding**: Heme iron binds oxygen in transport proteins, with protein structure controlling affinity and cooperativity.
- **Catalytic metal sites**: A bound metal can activate a substrate or water molecule, as zinc does in carbonic anhydrase.
- **Metal homeostasis**: Uptake, storage and delivery regulate metal availability while limiting inappropriate binding and chemical damage.
