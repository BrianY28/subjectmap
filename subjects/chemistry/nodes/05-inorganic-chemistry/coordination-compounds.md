# Coordination compounds

Metal centers bind ligands into complexes whose properties depend on composition and geometry. Coordination chemistry connects inorganic structure with catalysis, materials and biology.

## Concepts

- **Coordination sphere**: Ligand donor atoms bound to a central atom define its local coordination environment.
- **Denticity and chelation**: A ligand with multiple donor sites can bind through more than one site to form chelate rings.
- **Isomerism**: Complexes with the same formula can differ in connectivity or three-dimensional arrangement.
- **Stability versus lability**: Thermodynamic binding strength and the rate of ligand exchange are distinct properties.
