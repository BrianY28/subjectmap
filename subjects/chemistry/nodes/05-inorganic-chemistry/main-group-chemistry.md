# Main-group chemistry

Main-group chemistry explores the compounds of the s- and p-block elements. Valence patterns organize their behavior while size, polarity and structure create substantial variation.

## Concepts

- **Valence patterns**: Recurring valence-electron configurations help explain common bonding and oxidation-state patterns.
- **Oxides and acid-base behavior**: Many nonmetal oxides are acidic and many metal oxides basic, with amphoteric and other exceptions.
- **Allotropy**: An element can form different structures with distinct properties, as illustrated by graphite and diamond.
- **Stability and reactivity**: A compound’s thermodynamic stability and the kinetic barriers to reaction must be considered separately.
