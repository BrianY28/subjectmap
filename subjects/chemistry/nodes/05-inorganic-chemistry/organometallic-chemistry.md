# Organometallic chemistry

Organometallic compounds contain bonding between metals and carbon-containing groups. Their flexible bonding and reaction pathways support many catalytic transformations.

## Concepts

- **Metal-carbon bonding**: Carbon groups can attach through localized bonds or through delocalized π systems, as in sandwich complexes.
- **Electron counting**: Valence-electron counts help organize structures and reactivity, but the 18-electron rule is a useful pattern rather than a universal law.
- **Elementary transformations**: Oxidative addition, reductive elimination and migratory insertion provide recurring steps in organometallic mechanisms.
- **Catalytic cycles**: A sequence of elementary steps converts substrates into products while regenerating an active metal species.
