# Solid-state chemistry

Extended bonding and repeated structural motifs give solids properties that cannot be inferred from isolated molecules alone. Composition, defects and preparation history all matter.

## Concepts

- **Structural classes**: Ionic, molecular, covalent-network and metallic descriptions distinguish common types of solid bonding.
- **Lattices and unit cells**: A crystal structure specifies a repeating arrangement of atoms together with the lattice translations.
- **Defects**: Vacancies, substitutions and other departures from an ideal lattice can control transport and chemical behavior.
- **Structure-property links**: Bonding and electronic structure connect atomic arrangements to properties such as conductivity and mechanical response.
