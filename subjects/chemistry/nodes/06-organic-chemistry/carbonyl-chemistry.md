# Carbonyl chemistry

The polarized carbon-oxygen double bond provides both an electrophilic carbon and an oxygen with electron pairs. Neighboring groups determine which carbonyl pathways are accessible.

## Concepts

- **Nucleophilic addition**: A nucleophile can attack the carbonyl carbon to produce a tetrahedral arrangement before subsequent steps.
- **Acyl substitution**: Carbonyl compounds with suitable leaving groups can undergo addition followed by elimination that restores a carbonyl bond.
- **Enols and enolates**: An α hydrogen next to a carbonyl can enable enol or enolate formation and reactions at the adjacent carbon.
- **Carbon-carbon bond formation**: An enol or enolate reacting with another carbonyl compound provides the foundation of aldol chemistry.
