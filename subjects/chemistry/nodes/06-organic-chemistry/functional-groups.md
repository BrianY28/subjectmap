# Functional groups

Recognizable bonding patterns organize the properties and reactions of organic molecules. Their behavior is modified by the rest of the molecule and its environment.

## Concepts

- **Structural families**: Alcohols, amines, carbonyl compounds and other families are recognized by characteristic atoms and bonding patterns.
- **Electronic effects**: Inductive effects and resonance influence charge distribution, acidity and the availability of electron pairs.
- **Nucleophiles and electrophiles**: Electron-rich and electron-poor reaction sites provide a useful framework for many polar mechanisms.
- **Context matters**: Steric crowding, neighboring groups and solvent can change a functional group’s reactivity or selectivity.
