# Chromatography

Chromatography separates mixture components through different interactions with a moving phase and a stationary phase. Retention and detector response provide complementary information about identity and amount.

## Concepts

- **Partition and adsorption**: Different affinities for the two phases produce different migration speeds.
- **Resolution**: Separation depends on peak spacing and peak width, so a longer retention time alone does not guarantee a better separation.
- **Gas and liquid chromatography**: The choice of mobile phase and separation conditions depends on volatility, stability and analyte interactions.
- **Identification and quantification**: Retention time needs supporting evidence for identity, while concentration estimates require calibration and control of detector response.
