# Measurement and uncertainty

A measured value is meaningful only with its method, units and uncertainty. Sampling and calibration can dominate the error even when an instrument produces many decimal places.

## Concepts

- **Accuracy and precision**: Small scatter does not rule out a shared bias, so replicate measurements alone cannot establish accuracy.
- **Calibration**: A relation between signal and reference values lets an instrument estimate an unknown within a validated range.
- **Uncertainty propagation**: Combine relevant uncertainty contributions with their sensitivities and correlations rather than merely adding significant figures.
- **Representative sampling**: Sampling, blanks and reference materials test whether the result describes the intended material rather than contamination or a selected portion.
