# Batteries and fuel cells

Conventional batteries store chemical reactants within a device, while fuel cells receive reactants from outside during operation. Both convert redox free energy into electrical work and face limits from materials, transport and degradation.

## Concepts

- **Capacity and energy**: Capacity measures charge; stored or delivered energy also depends on voltage and operating conditions.
- **Rechargeable chemistry**: Charging reverses the intended cell chemistry, but side reactions and structural changes can cause irreversible loss.
- **Fuel-cell operation**: Continuous fuel and oxidant supply supports continued operation, with performance constrained by catalysts and transport.
- **Performance tradeoffs**: Energy density, power, lifetime, efficiency and material availability must be considered together.
