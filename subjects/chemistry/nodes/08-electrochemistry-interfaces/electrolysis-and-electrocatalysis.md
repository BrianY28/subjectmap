# Electrolysis and electrocatalysis

Electrolysis uses electrical energy to drive chemical conversion. Electrocatalysts alter reaction pathways and rates, while product selectivity also depends on electrolyte, potential and mass transport.

## Concepts

- **Faraday's law**: The theoretical amount of product is charge divided by nF for an n-electron reaction.
- **Faradaic efficiency**: The fraction of charge assigned to a particular product measures current selectivity, not overall energy efficiency.
- **Competing reactions**: Multiple electrode processes can share the current, so the intended reaction need not consume all delivered charge.
- **Interface and transport**: Adsorption, local pH and reactant supply influence measured activity and can obscure intrinsic catalyst behavior.
