# Oxidation and reduction

Redox chemistry tracks changes in electron allocation between reacting species. Oxidation states provide consistent bookkeeping but are not generally the actual charges on atoms.

## Concepts

- **Oxidation states**: Formal electron assignment identifies oxidation-state changes without claiming a literal transfer of whole electrons in every bond.
- **Coupled half-reactions**: Oxidation and reduction must balance transferred electrons as well as atoms and overall charge.
- **Oxidants and reductants**: An oxidant accepts electrons and is reduced, while a reductant supplies electrons and is oxidized.
- **Disproportionation**: One species can be both oxidized and reduced, yielding products with different oxidation states.
