# Carbohydrates and lipids

Carbohydrates and lipids supply structural diversity, energy storage and molecular recognition in living systems. Their behavior depends on stereochemistry, functional groups and interactions with water.

## Concepts

- **Sugar stereochemistry**: Stereoisomerism and ring formation create distinct sugars and anomers with different biological recognition.
- **Glycosidic bonds**: The positions and configurations of links between sugars shape the properties of oligosaccharides and polysaccharides.
- **Lipid diversity**: Triacylglycerols, phospholipids and sterols have different structures and roles rather than a single repeating polymer architecture.
- **Membranes**: Amphiphilic lipids assemble into bilayers whose fluidity and permeability depend on composition and conditions.
