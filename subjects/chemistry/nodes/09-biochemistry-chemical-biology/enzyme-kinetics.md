# Enzyme kinetics

Enzymes accelerate chemical reactions by providing catalytic pathways without changing the equilibrium constant of the catalyzed reaction. Rate measurements reveal mechanisms only within the assumptions of the chosen model.

## Concepts

- **Michaelis-Menten model**: For a simple mechanism under initial-rate and steady-state conditions, v = V_max[S]/(K_m + [S]).
- **Saturation**: At high substrate concentration the simple model approaches V_max = k_cat[E]_total as active sites become occupied.
- **Meaning of K_m**: K_m is the substrate concentration at half V_max in this model and is not generally identical to a binding dissociation constant.
- **Regulation and inhibition**: Inhibitors, cooperativity and allosteric effects can change measured rate behavior or require models beyond Michaelis-Menten kinetics.
