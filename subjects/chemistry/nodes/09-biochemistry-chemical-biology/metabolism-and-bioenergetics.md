# Metabolism and bioenergetics

Metabolism couples chemical reactions to maintain living systems away from equilibrium. Free-energy changes depend on cellular composition, so standard-state values alone do not determine pathway operation.

## Concepts

- **Reaction coupling**: A favorable net reaction can drive an unfavorable step when the steps are linked by a shared chemical mechanism.
- **ATP hydrolysis**: The net hydrolysis reaction can release free energy under cellular conditions; breaking a bond alone requires energy.
- **Redox carriers**: Molecules such as NAD⁺ and NADH transfer reducing equivalents between metabolic processes.
- **Chemiosmotic coupling**: An electrochemical ion gradient across a membrane can power ATP synthesis through ATP synthase.
