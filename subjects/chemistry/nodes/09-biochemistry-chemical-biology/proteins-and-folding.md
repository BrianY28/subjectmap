# Proteins and folding

A protein's amino-acid sequence and environment shape its accessible structures and functions. Many proteins form folded domains, while others contain functional disordered regions or switch between conformations.

## Concepts

- **Peptide backbone**: Amino acids are joined by peptide bonds, while side-chain chemistry adds charge, polarity and other interactions.
- **Structural organization**: Secondary structures assemble into three-dimensional folds and, for some proteins, complexes of several chains.
- **Folding landscape**: Hydrophobic effects, hydrogen bonding, electrostatics and conformational entropy together shape the accessible conformations.
- **Stability and dynamics**: Changes in temperature, pH or sequence can alter folding and aggregation, and a static structure does not describe every functional motion.
