# Polymer structure and properties

Polymer properties reflect chain chemistry, size, organization and motion over many length and time scales. The same repeat unit can yield different materials when processing or molecular architecture changes.

## Concepts

- **Glass transition**: The glass transition reflects a change in segmental mobility and depends on measurement timescale rather than behaving like a simple equilibrium melting point.
- **Crystallinity**: Ordered regions can coexist with amorphous regions, affecting stiffness, permeability and thermal behavior.
- **Tacticity and branching**: Stereochemical regularity and side branches influence packing and the mobility of polymer chains.
- **Viscoelastic response**: Stress and deformation depend on both elastic storage and time-dependent relaxation of chain motion.
