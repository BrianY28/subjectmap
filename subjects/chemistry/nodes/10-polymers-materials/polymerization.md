# Polymerization

Polymerization links monomers into macromolecules through different growth mechanisms. The mechanism controls chain length, architecture and how molecular mass evolves during conversion.

## Concepts

- **Chain growth**: An active chain end adds monomer repeatedly, with initiation, propagation and possible termination or transfer.
- **Step growth**: Reactive functional groups on monomers and oligomers combine, often requiring very high conversion for high molecular mass.
- **Molecular-mass distribution**: A polymer sample usually contains different chain lengths, characterized by averages and dispersity.
- **Architecture control**: Branching, crosslinking and copolymer sequence create structures with properties beyond those of an isolated repeat unit.
