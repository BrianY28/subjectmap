# Porous materials

Porous solids provide internal spaces for adsorption, separation and catalysis. Zeolites, activated carbons and designed frameworks differ in pore geometry, chemistry and stability.

## Concepts

- **Accessible porosity**: A pore contributes to a measurement or application only if the relevant molecules can reach it under those conditions.
- **Zeolites**: Crystalline frameworks with molecular-scale channels can distinguish adsorbates by size and interaction.
- **Metal-organic frameworks**: Metal nodes and organic linkers form extended coordination networks, some of which retain permanent porosity.
- **Practical performance**: Water tolerance, cycling stability, transport and regeneration cost matter alongside surface area or ideal adsorption capacity.
