# Supramolecular chemistry

Supramolecular chemistry studies assemblies held together by interactions between molecular components. Recognition and self-assembly depend on the environment and on competition between possible structures.

## Concepts

- **Molecular recognition**: Complementary shape and interactions can favor association, but binding also includes solvation and entropy costs.
- **Host-guest chemistry**: A molecular host selectively binds a guest with an equilibrium strength that depends on conditions.
- **Self-assembly**: Components can organize into larger structures through reversible interactions and molecular design.
- **Kinetic trapping**: An observed assembly need not be the most stable one if barriers prevent equilibration.
