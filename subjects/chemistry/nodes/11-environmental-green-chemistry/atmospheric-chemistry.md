# Atmospheric chemistry

Atmospheric composition changes through emissions, transport, radiation-driven reactions and removal. The same molecule can have different consequences depending on its location and chemical surroundings.

## Concepts

- **Photochemical reactions**: Absorption of sunlight can initiate reactions and generate reactive species in the atmosphere.
- **Radical cycles**: Reactive intermediates such as OH participate in chains that control the removal or transformation of many trace gases.
- **Ozone by altitude**: Stratospheric ozone absorbs ultraviolet radiation, while elevated ground-level ozone is a harmful air pollutant.
- **Aerosol chemistry**: Suspended particles exchange matter with gases and provide surfaces for reactions, influencing air quality and radiation.
