# Pollutant fate

A released chemical can move between environmental media, transform or persist. Exposure depends on this history, so detecting a substance alone does not determine its full environmental impact.

## Concepts

- **Partitioning and transport**: Solubility, volatility and sorption influence movement through air, water, soil and sediment.
- **Transformation**: Photolysis, hydrolysis and biological processes can form products with properties different from the starting compound.
- **Persistence**: A degradation timescale depends on medium and conditions, so a single half-life is not universal.
- **Bioaccumulation**: Uptake exceeding elimination can increase concentrations in organisms; biomagnification specifically describes increases across trophic levels.
