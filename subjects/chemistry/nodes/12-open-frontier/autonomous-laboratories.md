# Autonomous laboratories

Autonomous laboratories connect experiment selection, execution, measurement and feedback. Demonstrated systems operate within defined chemical spaces and objectives, while broader exploratory reliability remains a research problem.

## Concepts

- **Closed-loop experimentation**: Measured outcomes guide subsequent experiments rather than merely executing a fixed sequence.
- **Demonstrated scope**: Mobile-robot studies have optimized photocatalysts and combined synthesis with multiple analytical methods for selected exploratory workflows.
- **Measurement validity**: An apparently successful signal can hide an unexpected product, so orthogonal characterization and reproducibility checks matter.
- **Open question**: Can systems detect instrument or interpretation failures, transfer across laboratories and explore new chemistry with reliable human oversight?
