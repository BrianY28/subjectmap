# Circular polymers

Circular-polymer research seeks materials and recovery processes that preserve useful molecular resources through repeated use. Selective depolymerization and enzyme engineering have produced demonstrations, but their practical scope depends on the waste stream and full process.

## Concepts

- **Recovery pathways**: Mechanical reuse, chemical depolymerization and redesign address different polymers and can complement one another.
- **Enzyme engineering**: PET hydrolase studies, including a 2026 design study, improve tested enzymes without establishing equivalent performance across all plastics or industrial feeds.
- **Process boundaries**: Pretreatment, additives, mixed materials, monomer purification and energy input affect whether recovered material closes a useful loop.
- **Open question**: Can repeated recovery retain performance and deliver lower total impacts at realistic scale, rather than merely achieving conversion in a selected assay?
