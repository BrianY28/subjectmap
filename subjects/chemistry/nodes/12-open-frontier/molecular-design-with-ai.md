# Molecular design with AI

Models can propose molecules or predict aspects of their structures and properties. A useful designed molecule must satisfy several experimental requirements that a favorable predicted score cannot establish by itself.

## Concepts

- **Generative design**: Candidate generation can optimize chosen properties, but the result depends on representation, training data and objective design.
- **Structure prediction**: AlphaFold 3 predicts structures of diverse biomolecular complexes, which is distinct from measuring binding affinity, dynamics or cellular activity.
- **Multiple constraints**: Synthetic accessibility, stability, selectivity and function must be assessed together rather than optimizing one proxy without validation.
- **Open question**: Can models generalize to unfamiliar chemical space and produce reproducibly useful molecules through prospective experimental tests?
