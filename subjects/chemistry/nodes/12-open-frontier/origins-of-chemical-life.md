# Origins of chemical life

Prebiotic chemistry tests how nonliving chemical systems could have acquired the components and processes needed for life. Individual plausible reactions do not yet reconstruct a complete historical path to a self-maintaining, evolving system.

## Concepts

- **Building blocks**: Experiments explore plausible chemical routes to precursors of nucleic acids, peptides and membranes.
- **Connected chemistry**: A candidate pathway must account for compatible environments, available feedstocks and how products move between stages.
- **Information and compartments**: Replication, selection and boundary formation must eventually operate together rather than only in isolated demonstrations.
- **Open question**: How did chemical networks integrate energy use, heredity and compartment growth under conditions that could occur on the early Earth?
