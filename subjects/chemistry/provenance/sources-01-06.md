# Source checks: foundations and core chemistry

Drafted by Codex (AI) on 10 November 2026. This pass produced 36 topics and 144 concepts in branches 01–06. No human subject review was performed, and the `reviewed_by` fields remain empty.

## Approach

The text is an original, concise synthesis of established chemistry. References point to the relevant textbook sections, official definitions, or research records. Topic dates are broad historical orientation, not documented priority claims. No worked examples, illustrations, experimental protocols or extended passages were copied from textbooks.

For established fundamentals, the main reference is Flowers, Theopold, Langley and Robinson, *Chemistry 2e* (OpenStax, 2019). Specific section URLs were opened and their titles checked across measurement, atoms, bonding, thermodynamics, equilibria, kinetics, solids and coordination chemistry. Organic topics cite John McMurry, *Organic Chemistry*, 10th edition (OpenStax, 2023), with section pages checked for stereochemistry, functional groups, polar reactions, addition, substitution, aromaticity, carbonyl chemistry and synthesis.

## Scope of checking

The pass checked source identity and topical relevance, corrected common oversimplifications, and validated file structure. It was not an independent line-by-line expert audit of every claim. In particular, detailed electronic-structure qualifications, ligand-exchange kinetics and the statistical assumptions of reaction-network models would benefit from specialist review.

The drafts distinguish activities from concentration approximations, equilibrium from rates, resonance contributors from exchanging molecules, formal charges from physical charge density, and model predictions from universal rules.
