# Lagrangian mechanics

Lagrange's Mécanique analytique recast all of mechanics in generalized coordinates using L = T − V and the Euler-Lagrange equations, with no explicit forces needed. Constraints are built in, and conserved quantities follow from symmetries of the Lagrangian.

## Concepts

- **Generalized coordinates**: Any set of independent parameters describing the configuration replaces Cartesian coordinates and automatically respects holonomic constraints.
- **Action principle**: Physical paths extremize the action S = ∫L dt, from which the equations of motion follow as stationarity conditions.
- **Cyclic coordinates and conservation**: A coordinate absent from L has a conjugate momentum that is constant, an early form of symmetry-conservation reasoning.
- **Lagrange multipliers**: Constraints are enforced by adding multiplier terms to the equations, and the multipliers give the constraint forces.
