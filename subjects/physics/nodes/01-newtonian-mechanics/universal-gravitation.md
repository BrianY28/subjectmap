# Universal gravitation

Newton proposed that every mass attracts every other with F = G m₁m₂/r², deriving Kepler's elliptical orbits and the Moon's fall from one universal law. Cavendish's torsion-balance experiment (1798) measured the Earth's mean density, and G was derived from his data later.

## Concepts

- **Inverse-square law**: The 1/r² force yields closed conic-section orbits, uniting terrestrial fall and celestial motion under one rule.
- **Kepler's laws derived**: Equal areas and the harmonic relation T² ∝ a³ follow directly from the inverse-square force law.
- **Shell theorem**: A spherically symmetric mass attracts external points as if all its mass were at the center, and exerts no net force inside a hollow shell.
- **Cavendish torsion balance**: The 1798 experiment measured the tiny gravitational attraction between lead spheres and determined the Earth's mean density; G was derived from his data later.
