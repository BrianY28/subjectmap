# Ampère's force law

Ampère explained Ørsted's 1820 observation of a magnetic needle deflected by current and formulated the force law between current-carrying wires, with F/L = μ₀I₁I₂/(2πr) for parallel wires.

## Concepts

- **Ørsted's compass experiment**: In 1820 Ørsted found that a current-carrying wire deflects a nearby magnetic compass needle.
- **Ampère's circuital law**: The line integral of magnetic field around a closed loop equals μ₀ times the enclosed current.
- **Parallel-current forces**: Parallel currents attract when in the same direction and repel when opposite.
- **Current-element force law**: Ampère's integro-differential law gives the force between infinitesimal current elements, later superseded by field theory.
