# Coulomb's law

Coulomb used a torsion balance to show that the force between point charges is proportional to q₁q₂ and falls off as 1/r²: F = (1/4πε₀) q₁q₂/r².

## Concepts

- **Torsion balance**: Coulomb's torsion balance measured tiny electrostatic forces by balancing them against a twisted fiber.
- **Electrostatic 1/r² law**: The electrostatic force between point charges decreases with the square of their separation, establishing the first quantitative law of electricity.
- **Superposition of charges**: The net force on a charge is the vector sum of the forces from individual charges.
