# Electromagnetic waves

Hertz generated and detected radio waves with spark-gap apparatus, confirming Maxwell's prediction that light is an electromagnetic wave travelling at c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀).

## Concepts

- **Spark-gap oscillator**: A high-voltage spark drove oscillating currents in a dipole-like antenna, radiating electromagnetic waves.
- **Resonant receiver loop**: A small loop with a spark gap detected the arriving waves by resonant induction.
- **Reflection and polarization**: Hertz showed that the waves could be reflected, refracted and polarized like light.
- **Speed-of-light measurement**: Hertz used standing waves to measure the wavelength and inferred a speed close to that of light.
