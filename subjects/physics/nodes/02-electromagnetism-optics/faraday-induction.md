# Faraday induction

Faraday found that a changing magnetic flux through a circuit induces an electromotive force, ε = −dΦ_B/dt, linking magnetism and electricity.

## Concepts

- **Electromagnetic induction**: Relative motion of a magnet and coil or a changing current generates a transient current in the coil.
- **Magnetic flux**: Flux Φ_B is the surface integral of B, and its rate of change determines the induced EMF.
- **Lines of force**: Faraday introduced intuitive field lines to describe magnetic and electric action through space.
- **Faraday disk**: The homopolar generator produced a continuous current from a rotating conducting disk in a magnetic field.
