# Lorentz force law

Lorentz formulated the force on a charge moving in electric and magnetic fields, F = q(E + v×B), completing the classical dynamics of charged particles.

## Concepts

- **Force on moving charge**: The electric term qE accelerates a charge while the magnetic term qv×B acts perpendicular to velocity.
- **Charged-particle trajectories**: In a uniform magnetic field the Lorentz force produces circular or helical motion with cyclotron frequency qB/m.
- **Electron theory of matter**: Lorentz treated matter as composed of charged particles whose motion produced electromagnetic phenomena.
