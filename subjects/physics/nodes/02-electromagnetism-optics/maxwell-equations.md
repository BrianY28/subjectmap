# Maxwell equations

Maxwell's 1865 paper set out electromagnetism as roughly 20 component equations, adding displacement current so that ∇×B = μ₀J + μ₀ε₀∂E/∂t and predicting c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀).

## Concepts

- **Displacement current**: A changing electric field acts as a current source of magnetic field, making Ampère's law consistent with charge conservation.
- **Four field equations**: Maxwell's 1865 paper listed about 20 equations in component form; Heaviside's 1884 reformulation condensed them into the four vector equations relating E, B, charge and current.
- **Electromagnetic theory of light**: Maxwell identified light as a propagating oscillation of electric and magnetic fields.
- **Luminiferous aether**: Maxwell's waves were initially thought to propagate through a mechanical aether, later discarded.
