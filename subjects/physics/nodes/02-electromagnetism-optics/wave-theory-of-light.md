# Wave theory of light

Young's interference experiments revived the wave theory by showing that light from two slits produces alternating bright and dark fringes with spacing Δy = λL/d.

## Concepts

- **Double-slit experiment**: Young observed interference between light passing through two nearby slits, a signature of wave superposition.
- **Interference fringes**: Bright and dark bands arise from constructive and destructive superposition of light waves.
- **Principle of superposition**: Overlapping light waves add amplitudes, so their relative phase determines the resulting intensity.
- **Wave–particle debate**: Young's results challenged Newton's corpuscular theory and reopened the question of light's nature.
