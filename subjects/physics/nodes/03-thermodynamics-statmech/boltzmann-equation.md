# Boltzmann equation

Boltzmann's integro-differential equation describes how a one-particle distribution evolves under external forces and binary collisions: ∂f/∂t + v·∇_r f + (F/m)·∇_v f = (∂f/∂t)_coll.

## Concepts

- **Collision integral**: The right-hand side sums changes in f from binary molecular collisions.
- **Molecular chaos (Stosszahlansatz)**: Boltzmann assumed pre-collision velocities are statistically uncorrelated, introducing irreversibility.
- **H-theorem**: Boltzmann showed his H functional decreases monotonically, connecting microscopic dynamics to entropy growth.
- **Reversibility and recurrence objections**: Loschmidt's reversibility and Zermelo's recurrence objections exposed tensions between microscopic reversibility and macroscopic irreversibility.
