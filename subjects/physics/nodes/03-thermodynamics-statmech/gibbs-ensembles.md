# Gibbs ensembles

Gibbs formulated statistical mechanics through ensembles of microscopic states, with canonical probabilities P ∝ exp(-H/kT) and thermodynamic quantities derived from partition functions such as F = -kT ln Z.

## Concepts

- **Microcanonical ensemble**: An isolated system with fixed energy is described by a uniform distribution over accessible microstates.
- **Canonical ensemble**: A system in contact with a heat bath has P ∝ exp(-H/kT), yielding the Helmholtz free energy.
- **Grand canonical ensemble**: A system exchanging energy and particles with a reservoir uses a grand partition function and chemical potential.
- **Partition function**: The normalization Z encodes the statistical sum from which thermodynamic observables follow.
