# Kinetic theory of gases

Maxwell derived the molecular speed distribution f(v) ∝ v² exp(-mv²/2kT) and, with Boltzmann, connected macroscopic gas properties to microscopic collisions. Clausius had earlier introduced the mean free path.

## Concepts

- **Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution**: Molecular speeds follow f(v) ∝ v² exp(-mv²/2kT) in thermal equilibrium.
- **Mean free path**: Clausius introduced the average distance a molecule travels between collisions.
- **Equipartition theorem**: Each quadratic degree of freedom contributes ½kT to the average energy in classical thermal equilibrium.
- **Transport coefficients**: Kinetic theory relates viscosity, thermal conductivity and diffusion to molecular collisions.
