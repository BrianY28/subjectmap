# Statistical entropy

Boltzmann related entropy to the number W of microscopic configurations compatible with a macrostate, S = k log W. Planck later introduced the constant k and gave it Boltzmann's name.

## Concepts

- **Microstates and macrostates**: A macrostate's entropy counts the number W of microscopic configurations consistent with it.
- **Boltzmann entropy formula**: S = k log W links thermodynamic entropy to statistical multiplicity.
- **Boltzmann constant**: The constant k ≈ 1.38 × 10⁻²³ J/K was introduced by Planck in 1900.
- **Second-law interpretation**: Equilibrium corresponds to the overwhelmingly most probable macrostate, explaining entropy increase statistically.
