# Bohr atom

Bohr proposed quantized stationary electron orbits with angular momentum L = nħ, with radiation emitted only in transitions hν = E_i − E_f.

## Concepts

- **Stationary states**: Electrons occupy discrete orbits without radiating.
- **Hydrogen spectrum**: Transition energies reproduce the Balmer and Lyman series.
- **Bohr radius**: a₀ = 4πε₀ħ²/(m_e e²) ≈ 0.529 Å sets atomic size.
- **Correspondence principle**: Quantum predictions approach classical physics for large n.
