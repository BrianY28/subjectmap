# Dirac equation

Dirac's relativistic wave equation iħ∂ψ/∂t = (cα·p + βmc²)ψ predicted spin-1/2 electrons and antimatter.

## Concepts

- **Relativistic wave equation**: The first-order equation treats space and time on equal footing.
- **Spin-1/2**: The four-component spinor automatically includes electron spin.
- **Antimatter prediction**: Negative-energy solutions were reinterpreted as antiparticles; Carl Anderson discovered the positron in 1932.
- **Dirac sea**: Hole theory interpreted negative-energy states as antiparticles.
