# Entanglement and Bell's theorem

Einstein, Podolsky and Rosen argued in 1935 that quantum mechanics is incomplete; Bell showed in 1964 that any local hidden-variable theory obeys inequalities violated by entangled states.

## Concepts

- **EPR paradox**: The 1935 argument used entangled positions and momenta to question completeness.
- **Quantum entanglement**: Schrödinger coined the term for nonseparable correlations between subsystems.
- **Bell inequality**: Local hidden-variable correlations must satisfy bounds that quantum entanglement violates.
- **Bell tests**: CHSH (1969) made the theorem testable; Clauser (1972) and Aspect (1982) observed violations, loophole-free tests followed in 2015, and Aspect, Clauser and Zeilinger shared the 2022 Nobel Prize.
