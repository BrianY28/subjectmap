# Gauge field theory

Yang and Mills generalized local U(1) gauge invariance to non-Abelian SU(2), introducing self-interacting gauge bosons. The field strength is F^a_{μν} = ∂_μ A^a_ν − ∂_ν A^a_μ + g f^{abc} A^b_μ A^c_ν.

## Concepts

- **Yang-Mills theory**: Non-Abelian gauge fields have nonlinear self-interactions.
- **Local gauge invariance**: Demanding invariance under spacetime-dependent transformations generates gauge interactions.
- **Non-Abelian field strength**: The f^{abc} term makes gauge bosons carry gauge charge.
- **Gauge boson masses**: Unbroken gauge symmetry predicts massless bosons, motivating spontaneous symmetry breaking in the 1960s.
