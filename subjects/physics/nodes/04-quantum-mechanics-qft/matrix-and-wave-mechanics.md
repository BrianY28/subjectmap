# Matrix and wave mechanics

Heisenberg's matrix mechanics (1925) and Schrödinger's wave mechanics (1926) gave two equivalent formulations of quantum mechanics. The wave equation is iħ∂ψ/∂t = Ĥψ.

## Concepts

- **Heisenberg matrix mechanics**: Observables are noncommuting matrices; [x,p] = iħ.
- **Schrödinger equation**: The wavefunction evolves according to iħ∂ψ/∂t = Ĥψ.
- **Born probability rule**: |ψ|² gives the probability density for measurement outcomes.
- **Transformation theory**: Dirac and Jordan formalized the equivalence of matrix and wave mechanics.
