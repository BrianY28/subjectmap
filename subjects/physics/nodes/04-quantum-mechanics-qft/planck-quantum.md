# Planck quantum

Planck explained blackbody radiation by assuming oscillator energies are emitted or absorbed in discrete quanta E = hν, introducing the Planck constant h.

## Concepts

- **Blackbody radiation law**: Planck's law, fitted to Rubens and Kurlbaum's precise infrared measurements, interpolates between the Wien and Rayleigh–Jeans limits.
- **Ultraviolet catastrophe**: The name, coined by Ehrenfest in 1911, describes the classical Rayleigh–Jeans divergence at short wavelengths; Planck's 1900 derivation predates it.
- **Energy quantization**: Energy exchange occurs in units E = hν, where ν is frequency.
- **Planck constant**: h ≈ 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J s sets the quantum of action.
