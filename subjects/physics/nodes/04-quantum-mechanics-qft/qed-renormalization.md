# QED renormalization

Feynman, Schwinger and Tomonaga developed covariant QED calculations, and Dyson showed how to renormalize infinities into measured mass and charge. The electron anomalous moment a_e = (g − 2)/2 ≈ α/(2π) became a precision test.

## Concepts

- **Lamb shift**: The 1947 measurement of hydrogen 2S–2P splitting stimulated QED.
- **Renormalization**: Divergent parameters are absorbed into physical mass and charge.
- **Feynman diagrams**: Graphical perturbation rules organize QED scattering amplitudes.
- **Anomalous magnetic moment**: QED predicts g ≈ 2.0023 for the electron, matching experiment.
