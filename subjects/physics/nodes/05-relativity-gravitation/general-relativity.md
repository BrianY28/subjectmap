# General relativity

Gravity is the curvature of spacetime sourced by stress-energy, and free bodies follow geodesics. The field equations are Rμν − ½R gμν = 8πG Tμν/c⁴.

## Concepts

- **Equivalence principle**: Local free fall is indistinguishable from inertial motion, motivating curved spacetime.
- **Einstein field equations**: They relate spacetime curvature to energy-momentum via Rμν − ½R gμν = 8πG Tμν/c⁴.
- **Geodesic equation**: Free particles follow extremal proper-time paths, d²xμ/dτ² + Γμ_αβ dxα/dτ dxβ/dτ = 0.
- **Gravitational time dilation**: Clocks deeper in a gravitational potential run slower than clocks higher up.
