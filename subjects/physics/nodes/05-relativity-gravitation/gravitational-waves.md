# Gravitational waves

Accelerating masses radiate ripples in spacetime, predicted by Einstein in 1916 and first detected directly by LIGO on 14 September 2015. The strain h = ΔL/L measures fractional stretching of space.

## Concepts

- **GW150914 detection**: The first direct detection came from two black holes merging about 1.3 billion light-years away.
- **GW170817 neutron-star merger**: In 2017 LIGO/Virgo and about 70 observatories observed the same neutron-star merger in gravitational waves and light, a kilonova that launched multi-messenger astronomy.
- **LIGO interferometers**: Kilometre-scale Michelson interferometers detect differential arm-length changes below 10⁻¹⁸ m.
- **Nanohertz background**: Pulsar timing arrays including NANOGrav reported evidence in 2023 for a nanohertz gravitational-wave background.
