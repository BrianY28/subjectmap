# Mass-energy equivalence

Mass is a form of energy, with a body at rest having energy E₀ = mc². Mass defects in bound systems therefore appear as released energy.

## Concepts

- **Rest energy**: A particle at rest has energy E₀ = mc², even with zero momentum.
- **Relativistic momentum**: Momentum p = γmv preserves conservation laws at speeds near c.
- **Mass defect**: Binding energy lowers a system's mass by Δm = E_b/c².
