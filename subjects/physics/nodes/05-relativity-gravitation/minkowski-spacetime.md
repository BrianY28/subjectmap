# Minkowski spacetime

Space and time are unified into a four-dimensional spacetime with invariant interval s² = c²t² − x² − y² − z². This geometric formulation made Lorentz covariance manifest.

## Concepts

- **Four-vectors**: Position, momentum and current combine into Lorentz-covariant four-vectors.
- **Light cone**: The causal structure separates timelike, lightlike and spacelike intervals.
- **World line**: A particle's history is a curve through four-dimensional spacetime.
- **Spacetime diagram**: Events are plotted with time versus spatial axes to display causal relations.
