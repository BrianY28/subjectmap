# Special relativity

Physics is the same in all inertial frames and the vacuum speed of light c is invariant, so simultaneity and lengths become frame-dependent. The Lorentz factor is γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²).

## Concepts

- **Lorentz transformations**: They mix space and time between inertial frames while leaving the speed of light invariant.
- **Relativity of simultaneity**: Events simultaneous in one inertial frame need not be simultaneous in another.
- **Constancy of light speed**: All inertial observers measure the same vacuum speed c, independent of source motion.
- **Relativistic velocity addition**: Velocities combine as (u + v)/(1 + uv/c²), so no inertial frame reaches c.
