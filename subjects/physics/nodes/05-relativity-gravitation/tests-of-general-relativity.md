# Tests of general relativity

Eddington and Dyson's 1919 eclipse observations confirmed Einstein's predicted light deflection, the first of many precision tests. The deflection is δ = 4GM/(c²b) for impact parameter b.

## Concepts

- **Light deflection**: Starlight grazing the Sun is deflected by δ = 4GM/(c²b), twice the Newtonian value.
- **Perihelion precession**: General relativity explained Mercury's anomalous perihelion advance of about 43 arcseconds per century.
- **Shapiro time delay**: Radar signals passing near a massive body arrive later because of spacetime curvature.
- **Gravity Probe B**: Orbiting gyroscopes measured geodetic and frame-dragging precession around Earth.
