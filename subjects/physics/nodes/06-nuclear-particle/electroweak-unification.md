# Electroweak unification

Glashow, Salam and Weinberg unified electromagnetism and the weak force in a spontaneously broken SU(2)L × U(1)Y gauge theory, predicting W and Z bosons and neutral currents. The mixing angle relates the couplings through e = g sin θ_W.

## Concepts

- **Glashow SU(2)×U(1)**: In 1961 Glashow proposed the electroweak gauge group SU(2) × U(1).
- **Weinberg-Salam model**: In 1967 Weinberg and Salam incorporated spontaneous symmetry breaking into the electroweak theory.
- **Neutral currents**: Gargamelle observed weak neutral-current events in 1973, confirming the theory.
- **W and Z bosons**: CERN discovered the W and Z bosons in 1983 at masses predicted by the theory.
