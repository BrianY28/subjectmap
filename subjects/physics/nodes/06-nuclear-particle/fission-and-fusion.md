# Fission and fusion

Neutron bombardment of uranium produced barium and lighter elements, which Hahn and Strassmann reported as fission and Meitner and Frisch explained via the liquid-drop model and mass-energy conversion. Bethe's contemporaneous work identified fusion reactions as stellar energy sources, with E = Δm c² governing the energy release.

## Concepts

- **Hahn-Strassmann fission**: In 1938 they found barium among neutron-irradiated uranium products.
- **Meitner-Frisch explanation**: In 1939 they interpreted fission as liquid-drop splitting with large energy release.
- **Stellar fusion cycles**: Bethe's pp chain and CNO cycle in 1938-39 explained hydrogen fusion in stars.
- **Chain reaction**: Fission neutrons can sustain a self-amplifying nuclear chain reaction.
