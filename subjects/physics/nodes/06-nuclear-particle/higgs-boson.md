# Higgs boson

ATLAS and CMS discovered a scalar boson near 125 GeV/c² at the LHC, matching the Higgs boson predicted by the Brout-Englert-Higgs mechanism that gives masses to W and Z bosons and fermions. Its scalar potential is V(φ) = μ²φ†φ + λ(φ†φ)².

## Concepts

- **Brout-Englert-Higgs mechanism**: In 1964 several groups showed that spontaneous breaking of a gauge symmetry can generate particle masses.
- **ATLAS/CMS discovery**: In 2012 the two LHC experiments reported a new boson near 125 GeV/c².
- **Higgs couplings**: Observed production and decay rates are consistent with Standard Model mass generation.
- **Open questions**: The hierarchy problem, vacuum stability and possible Higgs partners remain unresolved.
