# Neutron and neutrino

Pauli postulated a neutral, nearly massless particle to preserve energy and momentum in beta decay, while Chadwick's neutron and Fermi's theory established the nuclear ingredients. Neutrinos were directly detected in 1956 through inverse beta decay, consistent with n → p + e⁻ + ν̄_e.

## Concepts

- **Pauli neutrino hypothesis**: In 1930 Pauli proposed a hidden neutral particle to explain the continuous beta-decay energy spectrum.
- **Chadwick neutron**: In 1932 Chadwick identified the neutral nuclear constituent with mass close to the proton.
- **Fermi beta-decay theory**: In 1934 Fermi treated beta decay as a weak four-fermion interaction.
- **Cowan-Reines detection**: In 1956 Reines and Cowan observed reactor antineutrinos via inverse beta decay.
