# Quantum chromodynamics

QCD is a non-Abelian SU(3) gauge theory in which quarks carry color and interact via eight gluons. The strong coupling α_s(Q²) falls as Q² rises, giving asymptotic freedom while confinement keeps isolated quarks unseen.

## Concepts

- **Color charge**: Quarks carry one of three color charges, while gluons carry color-anticolor combinations.
- **Gluon self-interaction**: Non-Abelian gluon fields carry color and interact with one another.
- **Asymptotic freedom**: Gross, Wilczek and Politzer showed in 1973 that α_s decreases at high momentum transfer.
- **Confinement**: No isolated colored particles are observed; hadrons are color singlets.
