# Quark model

Gell-Mann and Zweig independently proposed that mesons and baryons are composites of fractionally charged up, down and strange quarks, explaining SU(3) multiplet patterns. The proton is uud, giving charge +2/3 + 2/3 − 1/3 = +1.

## Concepts

- **Eightfold Way**: Gell-Mann and Ne'eman classified hadrons into SU(3) multiplets in 1961.
- **Fractional quark charges**: Quarks carry charges +2/3 or −1/3 and initially came in three flavors.
- **Hadron composition**: Baryons are three-quark states and mesons are quark-antiquark pairs.
- **Deep inelastic evidence**: Late-1960s SLAC scattering revealed pointlike charged constituents inside protons.
