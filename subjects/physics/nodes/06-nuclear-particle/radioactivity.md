# Radioactivity

Becquerel discovered penetrating radiation from uranium salts in 1896, and the Curies discovered polonium and radium in 1898. The decay law N = N₀e^(−λt) describes the random decay of unstable nuclei, with half-life t₁/₂ = ln 2/λ.

## Concepts

- **Becquerel's uranium rays**: In 1896 Becquerel found that uranium salts emit penetrating rays without external excitation.
- **Curie radium discovery**: Marie and Pierre Curie discovered polonium and radium in 1898, showing radioactivity is an atomic property.
- **Alpha, beta, gamma rays**: Rutherford classified alpha and beta rays by 1899, and Villard identified gamma rays in 1900.
- **Exponential decay law**: The decay rate proportional to N gives N = N₀e^(−λt) and a fixed half-life for each nuclide.
