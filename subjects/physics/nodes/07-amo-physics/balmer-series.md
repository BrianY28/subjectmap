# Balmer series

Balmer found an empirical formula fitting the visible hydrogen lines, 1/λ = R(1/4 − 1/n²) for n = 3, 4, ..., with R the Rydberg constant. Bohr derived it in 1913 from quantized electron orbits.

## Concepts

- **Balmer formula**: The formula predicts the wavelengths of the visible hydrogen series from a simple integer relation.
- **H-alpha line**: The n = 3 to n = 2 transition gives the red H-alpha line at 656.3 nm.
- **Rydberg formula**: Rydberg generalized the pattern to 1/λ = R(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²) for all hydrogen series.
- **Bohr explanation**: Bohr's quantized angular momentum L = nħ reproduced the Balmer formula and introduced atomic energy levels.
