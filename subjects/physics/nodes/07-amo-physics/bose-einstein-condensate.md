# Bose-Einstein condensate

Predicted by Bose and Einstein in 1924–25, a BEC was first realized in dilute atomic gases by Cornell and Wieman in rubidium-87 at JILA in 1995, followed by Ketterle's sodium condensate at MIT. Below a critical temperature, bosons macroscopically occupy the same quantum state.

## Concepts

- **Rubidium-87 condensate**: The JILA experiment cooled Rb-87 atoms in a magnetic trap to nanokelvin temperatures.
- **Laser cooling and trapping**: Magneto-optical traps and evaporative cooling, developed by Chu, Cohen-Tannoudji and Phillips (Nobel 1997), provided the ultracold samples that made BEC possible.
- **Macroscopic wavefunction**: The condensate is described by a single complex order parameter with coherent phase.
- **Gross-Pitaevskii equation**: Mean-field theory gives iħ∂ψ/∂t = (−ħ²/2m ∇² + V + g|ψ|²)ψ.
