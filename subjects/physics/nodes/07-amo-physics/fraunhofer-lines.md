# Fraunhofer lines

Fraunhofer mapped the dark absorption lines of the solar spectrum and labeled the principal ones, turning spectroscopy into a precision probe of matter. Kirchhoff and Bunsen later explained in 1859 that each element produces a characteristic set of lines.

## Concepts

- **Solar absorption spectrum**: Dark lines arise when cooler solar gases absorb specific wavelengths from the continuous photosphere.
- **Fraunhofer D lines**: The prominent D lines near 589 nm were later identified with sodium.
- **Diffraction grating**: Fraunhofer's gratings enabled accurate measurement of spectral line wavelengths.
- **Kirchhoff–Bunsen law**: Each chemical element has a unique emission and absorption spectrum, founding analytical spectroscopy.
