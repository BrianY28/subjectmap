# Laser

Maiman demonstrated the first laser, a flashlamp-pumped ruby rod, in 1960, using stimulated emission in a population-inverted medium. Schawlow and Townes had proposed the optical maser/laser concept in 1958.

## Concepts

- **Ruby laser**: Maiman's ruby laser emitted at 694.3 nm and established the laser as a practical device.
- **Population inversion**: Optical pumping creates more atoms in the upper laser level than the lower level.
- **Optical resonator**: Mirrors provide feedback and select longitudinal and transverse modes.
- **Ultrafast and attosecond lasers**: Chirped pulse amplification (Strickland and Mourou, 1985; Nobel 2018) delivered tabletop petawatt pulses, while high-harmonic generation produced the first attosecond pulses (Agostini, Krausz and L'Huillier; Nobel 2023).
