# Stimulated emission

Einstein introduced spontaneous and stimulated emission through A and B coefficients, showing that an incident photon can trigger emission of an identical photon. This process is the basis of masers and lasers.

## Concepts

- **A and B coefficients**: A₂₁ gives spontaneous emission, while B₁₂ and B₂₁ give absorption and stimulated emission rates.
- **Stimulated photon amplification**: The emitted photon has the same frequency, phase, and direction as the stimulating photon.
- **Population inversion**: A nonequilibrium state with more atoms in the upper level is needed for net stimulated amplification.
- **Einstein relations**: Detailed balance gives A₂₁/B₂₁ = 8πhν³/c³ and B₁₂ = B₂₁ for nondegenerate levels.
