# Zeeman effect

Zeeman observed that magnetic fields split spectral lines, and Lorentz explained the normal triplet using classical electron oscillators. The anomalous Zeeman effect later required electron spin and the Landé g-factor.

## Concepts

- **Normal Zeeman triplet**: The normal pattern has frequency shifts Δν = 0, ±eB/(4πmₑ), equivalent to energy shifts ΔE = μB B m_l.
- **Anomalous Zeeman effect**: Many lines split into patterns not explained classically, pointing to spin and angular-momentum coupling.
- **Landé g-factor**: The g-factor parametrizes the magnetic moment of a coupled atomic state.
- **Polarization selection rules**: The emitted components have polarizations fixed by the change in magnetic quantum number.
