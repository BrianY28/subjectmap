# Band theory and Bloch theorem

Bloch's theorem (1928) shows that electrons in a periodic potential have wavefunctions ψ_k(r) = e^{ik·r} u_k(r), so allowed energies form bands separated by gaps. Wilson's 1931 classification used filled and empty bands to explain metals, insulators and semiconductors.

## Concepts

- **Bloch theorem**: Periodic potentials produce Bloch waves whose crystal momentum k is a good quantum number.
- **Band gap and Brillouin zone**: Bragg scattering at zone boundaries opens gaps and organizes electronic states into bands.
- **Effective mass**: Near a band extremum, an electron responds as a free particle with mass m* set by band curvature.
- **Wilson metal-insulator classification**: Wilson (1931) distinguished metals from insulators by partially filled versus filled bands.
