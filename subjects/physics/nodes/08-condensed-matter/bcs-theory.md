# BCS theory

Bardeen, Cooper and Schrieffer (1957) showed that a phonon-mediated attraction binds electrons into Cooper pairs, which condense into a gapped ground state. The weak-coupling gap is Δ ≈ 2ħω_D exp[−1/(N(0)V)].

## Concepts

- **Cooper pairs**: Two electrons near the Fermi surface form a bound pair for an arbitrarily weak attractive interaction.
- **Phonon-mediated attraction**: Lattice polarization screens Coulomb repulsion and provides a retarded attraction between electrons.
- **BCS energy gap**: Breaking a pair costs energy Δ, producing an activated excitation spectrum and exponential specific heat.
- **Isotope effect**: T_c depends on ionic mass as M^{−α}, supporting the phonon mechanism.
