# High-Tc cuprates

Bednorz and Müller discovered superconductivity near 35 K in La–Ba–Cu–O in 1986, and cuprates soon exceeded the 77 K boiling point of nitrogen. The pairing mechanism and pseudogap remain central open questions.

## Concepts

- **Cuprate discovery**: Layered copper oxides show high Tc and strong electronic anisotropy.
- **d-wave pairing**: Phase-sensitive measurements indicate a sign-changing d_{x²−y²} gap.
- **Pseudogap**: A partial gap persists above Tc, suggesting competing or precursor order.
- **Beyond cuprates**: Iron-based superconductors (Hosono, 2008) and high-pressure hydrides such as H₃S at 203 K (Eremets, 2015) extended the search for higher Tc.
