# Phase transitions and RG

Continuous phase transitions show power-law divergences such as ξ ∝ |T − T_c|^{−ν}, with exponents set by universality rather than microscopic details. Wilson's renormalization group (1971) explains this via fixed points of scale transformations.

## Concepts

- **Universality classes**: Systems sharing dimension and symmetry have the same critical exponents.
- **Scaling hypothesis**: Near T_c, the free energy is a homogeneous function of reduced temperature and field.
- **Wilson renormalization group**: Iterative coarse-graining flows to fixed points and yields scaling laws.
- **Epsilon expansion**: Wilson and Fisher computed exponents near four dimensions in powers of ε = 4 − d.
