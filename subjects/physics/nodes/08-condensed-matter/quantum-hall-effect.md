# Quantum Hall effect

In a strong perpendicular magnetic field, a two-dimensional electron gas exhibits quantized Hall conductance σ_xy = ν e²/h and vanishing longitudinal resistance. Integer (1980) and fractional (1982) plateaus arise from Landau-level filling, disorder, and topological edge states.

## Concepts

- **Integer quantum Hall effect**: von Klitzing observed exact σ_xy = ν e²/h plateaus in 1980.
- **Fractional quantum Hall effect**: Tsui and Störmer observed ν = 1/3 in 1982; Laughlin's wavefunction (1983) described it.
- **Landau levels**: Cyclotron motion is quantized as E_n = ħω_c(n + 1/2).
- **Topological edge states**: Chiral edge modes and a Chern number make the quantization robust against disorder.
