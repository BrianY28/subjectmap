# Semiconductors and transistor

Doping controls electron or hole carriers, and a p–n junction rectifies current according to the Shockley diode equation I = I₀ (e^{eV/k_BT} − 1). Bardeen, Brattain and Shockley's 1947 point-contact transistor at Bell Labs launched solid-state electronics.

## Concepts

- **Doping and carrier type**: Donor or acceptor impurities make n-type or p-type semiconductors with tunable conductivity.
- **p–n junction**: A depletion region and built-in field give asymmetric current–voltage rectification.
- **Point-contact transistor**: The first transistor used two closely spaced metal contacts on germanium to amplify signals.
- **MOSFET scaling**: The metal–oxide–semiconductor field-effect transistor became the scalable building block of integrated circuits.
