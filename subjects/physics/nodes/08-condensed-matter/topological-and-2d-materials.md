# Topological and 2D materials

Graphene was isolated in 2004, and its low-energy electrons obey H = v_F σ·k, a massless Dirac equation. Topological invariants then classified gapped phases with protected edge states, while twisted bilayers (2018) produced flat bands and correlated phases.

## Concepts

- **Graphene Dirac cones**: The honeycomb lattice gives linear bands at the K and K′ points.
- **Topological insulators**: Bulk band inversion yields conducting edge or surface states protected by symmetry.
- **Bulk-boundary correspondence**: A bulk Chern or Z₂ invariant counts the protected boundary modes.
- **Moiré flat bands**: Twisted bilayer graphene hosts correlated insulators and superconductivity near the magic angle.
