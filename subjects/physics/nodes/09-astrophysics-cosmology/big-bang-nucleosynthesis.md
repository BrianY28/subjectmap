# Big Bang nucleosynthesis

Alpher, Bethe and Gamow (1948) showed that the hot early universe synthesized light nuclei, predicting roughly 25% helium by mass. Alpher and Herman also predicted the relic radiation left over from that epoch.

## Concepts

- **Alpher-Bethe-Gamow paper**: The 1948 calculation of primordial element abundances in a hot Big Bang.
- **Neutron-proton freeze-out**: Weak interactions decouple near 1 MeV, fixing the neutron-to-proton ratio at about 1/7.
- **Deuterium bottleneck**: Deuterium formation waits until the temperature falls to about 0.1 MeV, starting nucleosynthesis.
- **Primordial abundances**: Predicted H, D, ³He, ⁴He and ⁷Li abundances broadly match observation, with a lingering lithium problem.
