# Compact stars and black holes

Chandrasekhar's 1931 limit showed that white dwarfs above about 1.4 solar masses must collapse, and Oppenheimer and Snyder (1939) modeled continued collapse to a black hole. The Event Horizon Telescope imaged the shadow of M87* in 2019, confirming the black-hole picture.

## Concepts

- **Chandrasekhar limit**: White dwarfs above roughly 1.4 M_sun cannot be supported by electron degeneracy pressure.
- **Neutron stars and pulsars**: Jocelyn Bell Burnell and Antony Hewish discovered pulsars in 1967, confirming neutron stars supported by degeneracy pressure.
- **Oppenheimer-Snyder collapse**: The first relativistic model of gravitational collapse forming a black hole.
- **Event Horizon Telescope**: Its 2019 radio image of M87*'s shadow gave direct evidence for a supermassive black hole, followed by an image of Sgr A* in 2022.
