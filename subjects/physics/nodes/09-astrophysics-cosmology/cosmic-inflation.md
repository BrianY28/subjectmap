# Cosmic inflation

Starobinsky (1980) and Guth (1981) proposed a brief exponential expansion that solves the horizon and flatness problems and seeds cosmic structure. Linde, Albrecht and Steinhardt developed the slow-roll variants in 1982.

## Concepts

- **Horizon problem**: Regions of the CMB farther apart than the causal horizon nonetheless share the same temperature.
- **Flatness problem**: Inflation drives the density parameter Ω toward 1, explaining the near-flat geometry.
- **Slow-roll inflation**: A scalar field rolling slowly gives near-exponential expansion, a(t) ∝ e^{Ht}, and density perturbations.
- **Primordial B-modes**: An open search for inflationary gravitational waves imprinted on CMB polarization.
