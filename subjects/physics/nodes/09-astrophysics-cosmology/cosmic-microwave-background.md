# Cosmic microwave background

Penzias and Wilson detected an unexpected isotropic 2.7 K microwave background in 1965, quickly interpreted by Dicke's group as relic radiation from the hot Big Bang. COBE's FIRAS measured its blackbody spectrum in 1990 and its DMR found the tiny anisotropies in 1992 that seed structure.

## Concepts

- **Penzias-Wilson discovery**: The accidental 1965 detection of isotropic 2.7 K microwave noise at Bell Labs.
- **COBE blackbody spectrum**: The 1990 FIRAS measurement matched a Planck spectrum to extraordinary precision, T = 2.725 K.
- **CMB acoustic peaks**: WMAP and Planck map temperature anisotropies and pin down cosmological parameters.
- **Recombination and decoupling**: Neutral atoms formed near redshift z ≈ 1100, letting photons free-stream to us.
