# Dark energy and ΛCDM

In 1998 two supernova teams found distant Type Ia supernovae fainter than expected, implying accelerating expansion driven by dark energy with equation of state w ≈ -1. ΛCDM now fits CMB, supernova and BAO data, although local and early-universe H₀ measurements disagree.

## Concepts

- **Accelerating universe (1998)**: High-redshift Type Ia supernovae reveal that cosmic expansion is speeding up.
- **Cosmological constant Λ**: Vacuum energy with negative pressure, obeying w = p/ρc² ≈ -1, drives the acceleration.
- **ΛCDM model**: A six-parameter model with cold dark matter and Λ fits CMB, BAO and supernova data, H² = 8πGρ/3 + Λc²/3 - kc²/a².
- **Baryon acoustic oscillations**: The 2005 SDSS and 2dFGRS detections of the ~150 Mpc BAO feature provided a standard ruler for mapping the expansion history.
