# Hubble law

Hubble's 1929 observations showed that galaxy recession velocity grows linearly with distance, v = H₀d, evidence for an expanding universe. Lemaître had derived the relation and its expansion interpretation two years earlier.

## Concepts

- **Hubble constant H₀**: The present expansion rate, measured near 70 km/s/Mpc, with a persistent discrepancy between distance-ladder and early-universe determinations.
- **Distance ladder**: Cepheid variables and standard candles calibrate extragalactic distances step by step.
- **Lemaître's expanding universe**: In 1927 Lemaître derived v = H₀d and interpreted it as cosmic expansion, before Hubble's paper.
- **Redshift-distance relation**: Cosmological redshift of galaxy spectra grows with distance, a signature of metric expansion.
