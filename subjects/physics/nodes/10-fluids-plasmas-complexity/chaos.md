# Chaos

Nonlinear deterministic systems can display sensitive dependence on initial conditions, so nearby trajectories separate exponentially and long-term prediction fails. Poincaré found the geometry in the three-body problem (1890) and Lorenz exhibited it in a truncated convection model (1963).

## Concepts

- **Sensitive dependence**: A positive Lyapunov exponent makes small errors grow as δ(t) ≈ δ₀e^(λt), setting a finite predictability horizon.
- **Lorenz attractor**: Lorenz's 1963 three-variable convection equations produce a strange attractor of fractal geometry, popularized as the butterfly effect.
- **Homoclinic tangles**: Poincaré's 1890 work on the three-body problem found intersecting stable and unstable manifolds, a geometric mechanism for chaos.
- **KAM theorem**: Kolmogorov (1954) with Arnold and Moser (1962-63) proved most invariant tori survive small perturbations, delimiting where chaos appears.
