# Plasma physics and MHD

In ionized gas, long-range Coulomb forces make particles act collectively over the Debye length, supporting waves and instabilities. Ideal magnetohydrodynamics couples fluid momentum to Maxwell's fields: ρ(∂u/∂t + u·∇u) = J×B − ∇p with ∂B/∂t = ∇×(u×B).

## Concepts

- **Debye shielding**: Debye and Hückel (1923) gave the screening length λ_D = √(ε₀kT/ne²), the scale over which a plasma neutralizes charge imbalances.
- **Plasma oscillations**: Langmuir (1928) found collective electron oscillations at ω_p = √(ne²/ε₀m) and coined the term 'plasma' for the ionized state.
- **Alfvén waves**: Alfvén (1942) predicted transverse waves propagating along field lines at v_A = B/√(μ₀ρ), the cornerstone of MHD.
- **Magnetic reconnection**: Field lines break and rejoin, releasing magnetic energy in solar flares and tokamak disruptions; the fast reconnection rate remains debated.
