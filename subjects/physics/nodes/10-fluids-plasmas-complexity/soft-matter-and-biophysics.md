# Soft matter and biophysics

Soft matter is governed by thermal energy kT and entropy, so weak interactions and Brownian motion set mesoscopic structure. Canonical results include polymer scaling R_F ~ N^(3/5)a and reptation dynamics with relaxation time τ ~ N³.

## Concepts

- **Flory-Huggins theory**: Flory (1941) and Huggins described polymer solutions and blends with a lattice free energy governed by the interaction parameter χ.
- **Reptation and tube model**: de Gennes (1971) and Doi-Edwards (1978) model entangled chains sliding in a tube, giving τ ~ N³ stress relaxation.
- **Liquid crystals**: Onsager (1949) and de Gennes explained orientational order and its nematic-isotropic transitions in rod-like and molecular materials.
- **Single-molecule biophysics**: Optical tweezers, FRET and cryo-EM since the 1990s measure forces and conformational changes in individual proteins, DNA and molecular motors.
