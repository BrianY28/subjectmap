# Controlled fusion energy

Known: D–T fusion can exceed target gain, with NIF achieving first ignition in 2022 (3.15 MJ from 2.05 MJ) and a 2025 record of 8.6 MJ from 2.08 MJ of laser energy (target gain above 4), while JET's final D–T campaign in late 2023 produced 69 MJ (announced February 2024); the Lawson criterion nTτ_E ≳ 3×10²¹ keV s m⁻³ sets the target. Unknown: sustained net electricity, tritium breeding, materials and economics; ITER's revised baseline delays first plasma to 2034 and D–T to 2039, EAST held a high-confinement plasma for 1,066 s in January 2025 and WEST for 1,337 s in February 2025, and SPARC, Wendelstein 7-X and private tokamaks are the main programmes.

## Concepts

- **Lawson criterion**: Magnetic fusion needs nTτ_E above the D–T ignition threshold; inertial fusion uses areal density and ρR.
- **NIF ignition**: The 2022 shot produced more fusion energy than laser energy on target, and the April 2025 shot reached 8.6 MJ from 2.08 MJ, but wall-plug gain and repetition rate remain far off.
- **Tokamaks and ITER**: ITER aims at Q=10 but its revised baseline delays first plasma to 2034 and D–T to 2039; EAST (1,066 s, January 2025) and WEST (1,337 s, February 2025) set long-pulse records.
- **Private fusion**: Commonwealth Fusion's SPARC, TAE and others target net energy in the 2030s; no private plant has demonstrated net electricity.
