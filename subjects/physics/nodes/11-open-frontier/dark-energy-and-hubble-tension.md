# Dark energy and Hubble tension

Known: cosmic expansion accelerates, and ΛCDM fits CMB, BAO and supernovae with dark energy ~68% and w = p/(ρc²) ≈ −1; the decisive discovery was the 1998 supernova result. Unknown: whether w evolves and whether the Hubble tension is new physics or systematics; DESI DR2 (March 2025) combined with CMB and supernovae prefers evolving dark energy at 2.8–4.2σ, below discovery, DESI completed its five-year survey in April 2026 with final results expected in 2027, and JWST/CCHP estimates near H₀ ≈ 70 km/s/Mpc make the tension debated.

## Concepts

- **Cosmic acceleration**: Riess/Perlmutter 1998 found distant SNe Ia dimmer than expected; Λ has w = −1.
- **Hubble tension**: SH0ES gives H₀ ≈ 73 and Planck ≈ 67.4 km/s/Mpc, while Freedman's JWST/CCHP ladder gives ≈ 70; whether the discrepancy is new physics or systematics is debated.
- **DESI evolving dark energy**: DESI DR2 (March 2025) with CMB and supernovae favours w0waCDM over Λ at 2.8–4.2σ, below the 5σ discovery threshold; the five-year survey finished in April 2026 and final results are expected in 2027.
- **Euclid and Rubin**: Euclid (launched 2023) and Rubin/LSST, which released its first images in 2025, will map weak lensing and BAO to test w(z) and the Hubble tension.
