# Dark matter identity

Known: about 27% of the cosmic energy density is cold, collisionless dark matter, inferred from galaxy rotation, lensing, the CMB and structure formation (Ω_DM h² ≈ 0.12). Unknown: its identity; LZ's 2024 null result reached about 2×10⁻⁴⁸ cm² near 40 GeV, and the 2024 XENONnT and PandaX-4T detection of solar ⁸B neutrinos via coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering marks the neutrino fog now limiting WIMP searches.

## Concepts

- **WIMP direct detection**: Xenon and argon detectors see no nuclear recoils above background; LZ (2024) reached about 2×10⁻⁴⁸ cm² near 40 GeV, pushing WIMP–nucleon limits into the neutrino-fog regime.
- **Axion haloscopes**: ADMX and HAYSTAC scan microwave cavities for axion–photon conversion; only small mass windows are excluded.
- **Neutrino fog**: In 2024 XENONnT and PandaX-4T detected solar ⁸B neutrinos through coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering, the background floor that WIMP searches are now reaching.
- **MOND alternatives**: Milgrom's 1983 modified-gravity fits galaxy curves but struggles with clusters and the CMB; no alternative is accepted.
