# Neutrino masses and nature

Known: neutrinos oscillate, so at least two masses are nonzero; Super-Kamiokande (1998) and SNO (2001) established atmospheric and solar oscillations, and mixing angles and Δm² are measured. Unknown: absolute mass scale, ordering, Dirac versus Majorana nature and leptonic CP violation; KATRIN's 2025 limit is mβ < 0.45 eV, cosmology (DESI 2025 + CMB) gives Σmν below about 0.07 eV near the ~0.06 eV oscillation minimum, JUNO's June 2026 first results give world-leading solar-parameter precision but not the ordering, global fits mildly favour normal ordering (~2.2σ), and JUNO, DUNE, KATRIN and neutrinoless double-beta experiments target the remaining questions.

## Concepts

- **Neutrino oscillation**: The PMNS formula P(νe→νμ) = sin²2θ sin²(Δm²L/4E) fits solar, atmospheric, reactor and beam data.
- **Mass ordering**: JUNO's 2026 first results give world-leading precision on sin²θ₁₂ and Δm²₂₁ but not the ordering; global fits mildly favour normal ordering at about 2.2σ.
- **Majorana versus Dirac**: Neutrinoless double-beta searches (CUORE, LEGEND, KamLAND-Zen) have seen no signal, so whether the neutrino is its own antiparticle is open.
- **Absolute neutrino mass**: KATRIN's 2025 limit is mβ < 0.45 eV, while cosmology gives Σmν below about 0.07 eV; the absolute scale remains unknown.
- **Neutrino astronomy**: IceCube's discovery of high-energy astrophysical neutrinos earned Francis Halzen the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics, opening a new window on cosmic accelerators.
