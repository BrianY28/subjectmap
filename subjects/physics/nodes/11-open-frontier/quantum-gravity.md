# Quantum gravity

Known: general relativity and quantum field theory conflict at the Planck scale ℓ_P = √(ħG/c³) ≈ 1.6×10⁻³⁵ m, while holography supplies consistent toy models. Unknown: a background-independent, empirically confirmed 4D theory; string theory/AdS-CFT, loop quantum gravity, asymptotic safety and causal dynamical triangulation are the main programmes, and no decisive experiment exists—LIGO's 2015 detection only opened strong-field tests.

## Concepts

- **Holographic duality**: Maldacena's 1997 AdS/CFT correspondence equates a gravity theory in anti-de Sitter space with a boundary conformal field theory, giving the best-defined nonperturbative quantum-gravity models.
- **Black-hole information paradox**: Hawking's 1974 calculation suggested information loss; 2019 replica-wormhole derivations of the Page curve support unitarity in holographic models, but the real-world resolution is open.
- **Loop quantum gravity**: Ashtekar's 1986 variables and Rovelli–Smolin spin networks quantize geometry; deriving a 4D continuum limit and low-energy gravity remains open.
- **Planck-scale phenomenology**: Gamma-ray-burst and gravitational-wave data constrain Lorentz violation and extra dimensions, but no Planck-scale signal has been confirmed.
